# MeLearn artwork — neutral balance revision

These eight assets were edited from the previous generated set with the built-in ImageGen tool. Direction: neutral white/light gray as the majority of each frame, charcoal material detail and shadows, cobalt blue as a limited brand accent. Previous assets remain available as versioned files. This is UX prototype artwork, pending brand review.

## Newsletter banner

File: `newsletter-learning-path-v3.png`

```text
Use case: style-transfer. Edit target: the supplied MeLearn newsletter banner image. Preserve its exact panoramic layout: three learning cards and curving learning path at the far left, completion emblem and path at the far right, generous completely clear center for heading and email form. Change ONLY the art direction and colors to remove the blue wash: make the backdrop clean neutral paper white (#F8F9FA), the cards and emblem white with subtle neutral charcoal-gray edges and convincing soft gray/black shadows; reserve vivid MeLearn cobalt blue for the thin path and a few small emblem details, no more than about 15% of visible pixels. Remove the little clouds and leaf decorations so the composition feels calm, mature and custom art-directed. Keep the dimensions and center negative space. No text, no logo, no gradient, no photography, no yellow/orange/purple/green or warm beige. Ensure the central area remains high-contrast for dark text.
```
