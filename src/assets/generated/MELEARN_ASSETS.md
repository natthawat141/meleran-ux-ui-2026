# MeLearn artwork set

These are locally generated concept assets for the UX prototype, not approved production brand files. Generated with OpenAI ImageGen using a single commissioned-art direction: white and pale sky blue, vivid cobalt accents, layered paper-clay dimensional forms, subtle shadows, no stock photography, no embedded text. The Higgsfield Website Builder design recipe informed visual consistency and placement; Higgsfield did not generate these files. Existing MeLearn hero photography and logo are deliberately retained. The default cover for newly created courses is documented separately in `course-default-v1.prompt.md`.

## Newsletter background

File: `newsletter-learning-path-v2.png`

Prompt:

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: commissioned visual system for the melearn e-learning website, wide newsletter background, 3:1 landscape
Primary request: Art-direct an original editorial graphic illustration that feels made by one skilled in-house brand design team for a modern Thai learning platform. Visual metaphor: a continuous cobalt-blue learning path links a small stack of three clean course modules at the far left edge with a refined completion medallion at the far right edge. The path curves around the outer edges, leaving the middle 55% of the image calm and completely empty for a real HTML headline and centered email form.
Style/medium: high-end dimensional paper sculpture with precision-cut layered shapes and softly embossed ink, clearly graphic-design artwork rather than stock photography or literal office objects; balanced solid shapes and fine lines, controlled matte shadows, strong design hierarchy.
Color palette: bright white, pale sky blue, crisp cobalt brand blue only. No cream, yellow, orange, purple, green or earthy hues. Even pale blue background with no color gradient.
Composition/framing: ultra-wide 3:1 horizontal banner; valuable motifs toward the far left and far right, ample crop safety; uninterrupted quiet negative space in center. Flat studio view, polished visual identity work.
Constraints: no people, laptops, books, notebooks, headphones, photograph, email or envelope icon, text, letters, fake UI screenshot, badges, logos, watermark, random floating cubes, excessive decorative confetti, glow or gradients.
```
