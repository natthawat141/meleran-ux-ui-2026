# Instructor portrait cutouts

Fictional AI-generated portraits for the landing-page prototype, not real instructors or verified qualifications.

Tool: built-in image_gen, edit mode, transparent_background=true.
Input: instructor-mock-sheet.png.
Output: instructor-mock-cutouts.png (1536 × 1024 RGBA PNG).

## Exact prompt

Edit this existing fictional instructor contact sheet. Remove all classroom backgrounds completely and return a genuine transparent RGBA PNG, no white backdrop, no checkerboard baked into pixels, no circles or text. Preserve the exact THREE fictional people, their faces, hair, casual blue clothes, notebooks/tablet, poses, and smiles from the original. Arrange them in the same left-to-right order (woman with blue notebook; man with glasses and tablet; woman with sketchbook) in THREE equal-width vertical columns on a wide 3:2 transparent canvas. Each person's complete hair and head, shoulders, arms and waist must fit fully WITHIN their own third with transparent margins on both sides and at least 6% canvas-height transparent margin above every head. No body or notebook crosses a column boundary. Portraits aligned at a common waist-height bottom edge with similar head heights. Carefully preserve fine hair edges and natural photographic texture. The images will be individually cropped from each third and layered in CSS over blue circles, with their heads projecting above each circle. Do not crop heads, add persons, alter identities, add shadows behind the people, add logos or text, or add decorative shapes. Output only the transparent cutout contact sheet.
